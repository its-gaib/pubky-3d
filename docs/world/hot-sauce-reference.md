# Bitkit Hot Sauce

The giant chili is an original procedural sculpture, lying on the ground at
`[-108, -103]` in its own northwest clearing, reached by a trail from Pubky University.
The red body and green crown span about 35 meters along the ground; a low plaque
replaces the original pedestal and remains reachable from the inward approach.
Its interaction displays the fourth image requested by the user:
**FIAT MELTDOWN**, from the [Bitkit Wallet post](https://x.com/bitkitwallet/status/2095148827699274000/photo/4).

The post is an image poll. Its images appear in the official X syndication
response under `card.binding_values`, rather than the usual tweet media array.
The exact post ID is `2095148827699274000`; `choice4_label` is `FIAT MELTDOWN`
and `choice4_image_original` supplies the [original JPEG](https://pbs.twimg.com/card_img/2095148599600418816/UQA73XrX?format=jpg&name=orig).
The [official source response](https://cdn.syndication.twimg.com/tweet-result?id=2095148827699274000&lang=en&token=1)
was checked on September 11, 2026.

The unchanged 1024 × 1024 image is bundled at
`public/world/bitkit-hot-sauce-fiat-meltdown.jpg` (342,985 bytes).
The app loads this local asset and uses fixed links to the original photo and
`https://x.com/bitkitwallet`. It does not load an X embed, script, or live post HTML.
The panel invites visitors to follow Bitkit Wallet for updates about free sauce
availability; it does not claim a current pickup place or schedule.
